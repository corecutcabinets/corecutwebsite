How we work

Our Process

From the first consultation to the final walkthrough, each step is kept clear so your cabinetry project stays organized before anything moves into production.

  1. 01

    Initial Consultation

    We talk through the space, project goals, rough budget, timeline, and the type of cabinetry you need. We also collect your floorplans and discuss materials, door styles, and hardware.

  2. 02

    3D Rendering Review

    You review the design visually so layout, proportions, finishes, and any necessary revisions can be confirmed before approval.

  3. 03

    Final Approval

    Once the design, details, and quote are approved, the project moves into final drawings and production planning.

  4. 04

    Production

    Cabinet parts are cut, finished, assembled, and checked for fit and quality before installation.

  5. 05

    Installation

    The cabinetry is installed on site with attention to alignment, function, and a clean finished result.

  6. 06

    Final Walkthrough & Service

    We review the completed project with the client, check adjustments, answer questions, and make sure everything is finished properly.
