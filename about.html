About Corecut Cabinets

Corecut Cabinets is an Edmonton-based father and son cabinetry company specializing in custom residential and commercial cabinetry.

What started as a family-run business was built on a simple goal: provide customers with quality cabinetry, honest service, and a smooth experience from start to finish.

We design, build, and install cabinetry for kitchens, bathrooms, offices, retail spaces, built-ins, and custom storage solutions. Every project is planned around the customer’s space, style, and everyday needs.

At Corecut Cabinets, quality starts at the core. From the initial consultation and measurements to material selection, 3D layout review, production, and installation, we focus on precision, clear communication, and workmanship that lasts.

Serving Edmonton and surrounding areas, we are committed to giving every customer the best service possible and cabinetry they can feel confident in.
