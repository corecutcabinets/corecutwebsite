Installation & service

Professional Cabinet Installation & Service in Edmonton

A clean, organized installation process that brings your custom cabinetry from design to final fit with precision, care, and clear communication.

Plan Your Installation

Where planning becomes finished work

Installation is where design, fabrication, and site preparation come together.

At Corecut Cabinets, installation is where design, craftsmanship, and planning come together. Our installation process is built around precision, organization, and respect for your space. From delivery coordination to final adjustments, our team works carefully to make sure your cabinetry is installed properly and finished with attention to detail.

Whether the project is kitchen cabinet installation in Edmonton, closet storage, a TV feature wall, garage cabinetry, or commercial millwork installation, the goal is the same: clean alignment, reliable function, and clear communication from start to finish.

Installation process timeline

A clear path from confirmation to final walkthrough.

Step 1

Pre-Install Confirmation

Step 2
