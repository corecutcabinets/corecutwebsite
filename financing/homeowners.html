Homeowner FAQs

Clear answers before you explore an option.

Complete rates and terms are provided during the official application process.

How much financing may be available?

Available financing amounts vary by project and applicant. The independent financing provider confirms eligibility and the approved amount after reviewing the official application.

Does the calculator affect my credit or guarantee approval?

No to both. The calculator only uses the figures you enter; it does not request a credit report or submit an application, and its estimates are for illustration only. Eligibility, approval, rates, fees and payment schedules are determined by the independent financing provider, and any credit check is disclosed before you submit an official application.

Is a down payment required?

A down payment may or may not be required depending on the available program, your application, and the project. Any requirement will be shown with the complete financing terms before you decide.

Can I pay the financing off early?

Yes. You can pay the financing off early without a prepayment penalty. Your financing agreement will explain how to request a payoff amount.

When will I receive my final rate and payment?

Your final rate, fees, payment amount, and schedule will be provided by the independent financing provider after it reviews your official application and before you accept a credit agreement.
