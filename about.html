Our Story

Corecut Cabinets is a family-owned cabinet company built on trust, quality workmanship, and treating every customer with respect.

Our family has years of experience in the cabinet industry, and we started Corecut Cabinets with a clear goal: to provide homeowners, builders, and businesses with dependable cabinetry and a straightforward experience from start to finish. We believe customers should feel confident knowing who they are working with, what they are paying for, and when their project will be completed.

We take pride in being involved throughout the entire process, from design and fabrication to delivery and installation. Every project is approached with care, attention to detail, and a commitment to building cabinets that are made to last.

As Corecut Cabinets continues to grow, our focus will remain the same: building long-term relationships, earning our customers’ trust, and becoming a reliable name for cabinetry in Edmonton and beyond.
