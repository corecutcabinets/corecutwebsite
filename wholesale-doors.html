Door range

Doors and drawer fronts, cut to your list.

Every door is cut from MDF to the exact sizes on your order, so it drops straight onto your boxes.

Shaker Doors

Classic five-piece look machined from one MDF sheet, so there are no joints to crack through the paint.

Shaker Drawer Fronts

Matching drawer fronts with the same frame width, ready to pair with your doors on the same order.

Slab Doors & Fronts

Flat MDF doors and fronts with clean eased edges for modern kitchens, vanities and built-ins.

Unfinished

Sanded & Painted

Sanded, primed and painted, ready to hang on your boxes.
