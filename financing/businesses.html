Business & builder FAQs

What to know before an official application.

Availability and complete terms depend on the independent financing provider and are subject to approval.

Who can apply, and what projects qualify?

Financing may be available to qualified builders, general contractors, renovation companies, property managers, developers, commercial businesses, dealers, and trade partners, including for multi-unit or phased cabinetry projects. Eligibility is determined by the independent financing provider.

How much financing may be available?

Available business financing amounts vary with the application, project, and independent financing provider. The approved amount is confirmed after the official application is reviewed.

What repayment choices may be available?

Qualified applicants can choose fixed weekly payments over 12, 26, or 52 weeks. Complete payment schedules, rates, fees, and conditions are provided before accepting an agreement.

What is required, and how long does review take?

An official application may request business identity, ownership, operating history, project details, and financial information; the provider identifies required documents before submission. Instant approval decisions are available for qualified applicants on some eligible, complete applications, and review timing otherwise varies with the provider and the amount requested.

Will checking my available options affect my credit?

Using this website calculator does not affect your credit because it does not access a credit report or submit an application. Any credit check connected to an official business financing application depends on the financing provider and will be disclosed before you submit.

Is Corecut the lender, and where do I see full terms?

No. Corecut Cabinets supplies cabinetry and helps business customers explore payment options. Financing is provided by an independent third-party financing provider, which discloses the complete rates, fees, payment schedule and conditions in the official application or credit agreement before you accept an option.
